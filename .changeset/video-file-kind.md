Videos join Media, within the Supabase Free plan. `videoFile({ label: 'Video', folder: 'proyectos' })` is a built-in field kind that stores a video of the library like `file` stores a PDF. The uploader takes mp4 and webm up to 25 MB (the bucket now allows the 50 MB the Free plan does per file; images and PDFs keep their 10 MB), reads their size and duration in the browser, and turns away an mp4 that is not faststart, with the ffmpeg flag that fixes it, because the browser would download the whole file before showing a frame. The library filters by "Videos" and shows each video by its poster with its duration; the details play the file, say what a thousand full plays cost in egress, and let the editor pick the poster from the images of Media or capture a frame, which becomes an image of the library in the video's folder. A video without a poster is missing content ("Portada del video de …"), so publishing waits for it, and the poster of a live video can't go to the trash (the panel names the video, the database refuses it too). The migration adds `duration` and `poster_id` to `media` and the video mime types to the bucket. On the site, a `videoFile` field resolves to a `SiteVideoType` with its `poster` as a `SiteImageType`, and `<Video video={clip} />` from `@linkcharsoft/cms/site` renders a `<video>` with `preload="metadata"`, `playsInline`, controls and the poster through the image optimizer; `autoplay` plays it muted and looping. `core` exports `videoFile`, `VideoFileField`, `VIDEO_MIME_TYPES`, `MAX_VIDEO_BYTES`, `isVideoMime`, `mediaTypeOf`, `formatDuration`, `egressGigabytes` and `readMp4Head`; `MediaItemType` gains `duration`, `posterId` and `posterUrl`, `MediaUsageType` a `kind` (`document` or `poster`), `updateMedia` takes `posterId` and `MissingItemType.kind` can be `poster`. Media is for the videos editors rotate; a site's own hero loop stays in `public/`, since Storage serves a video whole on every play.

To update a project: `linkchar-cms migrations sync` and `supabase db push`; then use `videoFile()` where the schema needs it and render it with `<Video>`.
