The address of a published document (`slug`) no longer follows its title: retitling "Casa" to "Casa grande" used to change a live URL without a word. Once a document has been published, the address only changes by hand or with "Generar de nuevo", which now regenerates it once from the title and says that the old address will redirect to the new one. Drafts never published keep following the title.

To update a project: nothing.
