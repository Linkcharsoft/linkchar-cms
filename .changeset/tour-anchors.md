Guided tours anchor their cards to small elements, each with a fixed side: the first section's header or the first field instead of the whole form, the first row instead of the whole list, the summary and the history title on Publish, and the header buttons get their card right-aligned under them. Before, a wrapper taller than the viewport sent the card to the bottom center and the editor's sticky bar covered the top of the form. The unused `users-invite` anchor is gone.

To update a project: nothing.
